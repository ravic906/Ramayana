## Rama's body and animations: Quaternius (CC0)

Both packs are released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain) by Quaternius

### What we derived
- `characters/rama/model/rama_body.scn` is generated by `tools/build_rama.gd`. The tool:
  - paints the outfit regions (pitambara, zari trim, uttariya) into vertex-colour masks
  - attaches the long hair to the head bone
  - keeps only the animation clips the game uses
- `characters/rama/model/textures/`: a greyscale skin detail map (stubble softened, underwear
  flattened), the body normal map, the eye texture and the hair detail map. These are resized
  or recoloured from the pack textures.
- Ornaments (kireeta, jewellery, dhoti pleats, quiver, the Kodanda bow) are procedural, in
  `characters/rama/rama_model.gd`.

### Rebuilding the body
1. Download both `[Standard]` zips from the links above and unzip them.
2. From the project root, run:
   ```
   godot --headless --import
   godot --headless -s res://tools/build_rama.gd -- "<…>/Universal Base Characters[Standard]" "<…>/Unreal-Godot/UAL1_Standard.glb"
   ```

## Animals: Quaternius Ultimate Animated Animals (CC0)
- https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html (CC0 1.0, by Quaternius)
- Used: `Deer`, `Stag`, `Bull` (glTF). `tools/build_animals.gd` scales them to life size, welds
  and smooth-shades the low-poly meshes, mutes the colours a little, adds LODs and keeps the
  clips the game uses (`characters/animals/model/`).

## Voices: Piper text-to-speech (placeholder)
`assets/audio/voice/*.ogg` are generated by `tools/gen_voices.py` with Piper
(https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) and its `en_GB-vctk-medium` voice. That voice is trained on
the CSTR VCTK Corpus (University of Edinburgh, CC BY 4.0) and fine-tuned from Piper's "lessac"
voice.

Before a commercial release, either record the cast or confirm the licence chain for this voice
model. The lessac source data has its own terms.

## Sound effects
`assets/audio/sfx/*.wav` are synthesised from scratch by `tools/gen_sfx.py`, so there are no
third-party sounds. The bowstring uses Karplus–Strong synthesis; the creak uses stick-slip
clicks through wooden resonances.

These are **prototype assets**. Final hero art will follow the Art Bible and replace them.
